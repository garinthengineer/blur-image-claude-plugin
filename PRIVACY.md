# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy covers the Blur Image plugin for Claude. It does not cover the Blur Image
extension for Chrome, which is a separate product.

## What the plugin does with your image

The plugin is a skill and a Python script. It has no server, no account, no API key and
makes no network requests of its own.

When you ask Claude to blur or pixelate a picture, the image you attached is already part
of your conversation with Claude. The script runs inside Claude's own sandbox, reads that
file, writes a blurred or pixelated copy, and hands the copy back to you in the same
conversation. Nothing leaves that conversation because of this plugin.

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. The plugin keeps no copies, writes no logs, uses no analytics, and sends no data
to the plugin's author or to any third party. It does not read anything else on your
computer or in your account.

## What governs the conversation itself

Your conversation with Claude, including any file you attach to it, is handled by
Anthropic under [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and
the terms of your Claude plan. This plugin does not change how Anthropic handles it.

## Children

The plugin is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Claude's consumer
terms require users to be at least 18 years old, or older where local law sets a higher
age.

## Changes

Any change to this policy is published in this file, in the plugin's public repository at
https://github.com/garinthengineer/blur-image-claude-plugin, with the date above updated.
